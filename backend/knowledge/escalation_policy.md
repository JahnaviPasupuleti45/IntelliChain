# Risk Escalation Policy

Applies to: all

Sample policy written for the IntelliChain demo. It explains what operations staff must do for each risk level the model assigns.

## LOW risk
No action is needed. The shipment continues as planned and readings are reviewed in the daily report.

## MEDIUM risk
The operations team must review the shipment within 4 hours. Contact the driver or carrier to confirm conditions, and check the next readings to see whether the risk is rising or falling. Escalate to HIGH handling if two medium-risk readings in a row show conditions getting worse.

## HIGH risk
The shift supervisor must be notified immediately. Within 1 hour, the team must:
1. Contact the carrier and confirm the problem (equipment failure, route delay or sensor fault).
2. Follow the product-specific SOP (pharma, food or electronics).
3. Inform the customer if the delivery time or product quality may be affected.
4. Log the incident with the shipment ID, the readings and the actions taken.

## Sensor fault check
If a reading looks impossible (for example a jump of more than 15 °C within a few minutes), check the sensor before escalating. Ask the driver for a manual thermometer reading to confirm it.
