# Food and Perishables Handling SOP

Applies to: food

Sample standard operating procedure written for the IntelliChain demo.

## Food temperature range
Fresh and chilled food must stay between 0 °C and 15 °C. Humidity up to 85 % is acceptable for fruit and vegetables. Dairy, meat and seafood should be kept below 5 °C.

## Food temperature excursion response
If a food shipment goes above 15 °C:
1. Record the time the excursion started and the highest temperature reached.
2. If the shipment stayed above 15 °C for more than 2 hours, the receiving warehouse must inspect it before accepting it.
3. Meat, dairy and seafood that stayed above 15 °C for more than 4 hours must be rejected and disposed of under food safety rules.

## Food humidity and condensation
Humidity above 85 % causes condensation, mould and packaging damage. Increase ventilation in the container and inspect the cartons on arrival. Mark wet or damaged cartons for return.

## Food delay handling
Perishables tolerate up to 4 hours of delay. For longer delays, inform the customer of a new delivery time and prioritise the shipment at the next hub so it is unloaded first.
