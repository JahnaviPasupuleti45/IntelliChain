# Pharmaceutical Cold Chain SOP

Applies to: pharma

Sample standard operating procedure written for the IntelliChain demo.

## Pharma temperature range
Vaccines, insulin and other temperature-sensitive medicines must stay between 2 °C and 8 °C for the whole journey. Humidity should stay below 60 %. Any reading outside this range is a temperature excursion and must be logged.

## Pharma temperature excursion response
If a pharma shipment reads above 8 °C or below 2 °C:
1. Notify the quality assurance (QA) team within 30 minutes.
2. Ask the driver to check that the refrigeration unit is running and the doors are sealed.
3. Move the shipment to the nearest certified cold storage facility if the excursion lasts longer than 1 hour.
4. Do not deliver the goods until QA has approved their release. QA decides whether the stock is quarantined or destroyed.

## Pharma freezing risk
Readings below 0 °C are critical for most vaccines because freezing destroys them even if the temperature recovers. Treat any sub-zero reading as HIGH risk and quarantine the shipment on arrival.

## Pharma delay handling
Pharma shipments tolerate at most 2 hours of delay. For longer delays, confirm the remaining cooling capacity of the container and arrange a backup vehicle if it will run out before delivery.
