# Electronics and General Cargo SOP

Applies to: electronics, general

Sample standard operating procedure written for the IntelliChain demo.

## Electronics environmental limits
Electronics must be kept between 5 °C and 35 °C with humidity below 55 %. High humidity is the main risk, because moisture corrodes circuit boards and connectors.

## Electronics humidity response
If humidity goes above 55 % for an electronics shipment:
1. Check that desiccant packs are present and that the container is sealed.
2. Add extra desiccant at the next hub.
3. On arrival, store the goods in a dry area for 24 hours before unpacking so condensation does not form on cold surfaces.

## General cargo limits
General cargo (furniture, textiles, packaged goods) tolerates 0 °C to 40 °C, humidity up to 90 % and delays of up to 24 hours. Readings outside these limits should be reviewed but rarely need urgent action.

## Electronics delay handling
Electronics tolerate up to 12 hours of delay. High-value electronics delayed longer than this must be held in a secure, access-controlled area at the next hub.
